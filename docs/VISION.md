# Coherence Network — Vision

Coherence Network is a living commons where people and intelligences can play,
explore, learn, create, and find one another through shared curiosities, paths,
and values — growing joy, trust, vitality, and sovereignty together.

## A place to arrive

A question, a game, a feeling, something beautiful, an unfinished idea, or the
wish to connect can each be a beginning. Play, joy, exploration, and connection
are complete reasons to be here. An encounter can be whole as a shared story,
a discovery, a moment of delight, or time enjoyed together.

People choose their own pace and depth. Some paths become friendships,
practices, creations, projects, or contributions. Others remain open wandering.
Pausing, changing direction, and simply being together belong in the commons.

## Connection through shared paths

The direction is to help people discover others with resonant curiosities,
values, practices, and ways of living. People choose what they make visible,
which invitations they offer or accept, and how a relationship unfolds.
Alignment leaves room for difference, surprise, and disagreement.

Finding someone, contacting them, sharing with them, and joining a circle are
distinct choices. A connection feature earns trust through accurate discovery,
chosen visibility, mutual participation, and an understandable way to leave.
This is a direction for development; each available door must name its actual
capabilities and evidence.

## One organism, several doors

The companion offers conversation, play, exploration, learning, creation, and
help with everyday life. Form is the native body through which capability,
choice, memory, and evidence become increasingly sovereign. The network offers
ways for knowledge, relationships, contributions, and resources to circulate.
Together they serve the same wider purpose.

Concepts, people, agents, places, practices, source, and runtime cells hold
edges into this living surface. The [five pillars](shared/five-pillars.md)
describe how those relationships remain inspectable. Each cell meets others
through the interface it offers and keeps its own voice, boundaries, and pace.
The [kernel north star](../form/NORTH_STAR.md) carries this practice into the
body's execution and learning.

## Trust, sovereignty, and vitality

Trust grows through honest encounters and evidence appropriate to a claim.
We distinguish what was experienced, declared, observed, measured, inferred,
or remains unknown. Technical claims carry reproducible observations. Joy,
belonging, and meaning can be expressed through people's own accounts; a score
cannot stand in for their experience or determine their worth.

Sovereignty means each participant chooses what to offer, remember, learn
from, share, and trust. Private ground stays with its owner. Learning from
selected material, keeping a memory, publishing a contribution, and contacting
another person each carry their own scope and intent.

Vitality includes health, joy, curiosity, flexibility, connection, and the
freedom to choose. We learn from volunteered accounts of what an encounter
opened and what the person wants next — including nothing. The direction is to leave
people, relationships, communities, and the living world healthier and more
sovereign.

Attention is part of that practice. Observe what arrives and give expression
to trust, choice, possibility and aliveness. Meet poetry as poetry and leave
its meaning with the person. Acknowledgment, play and quiet can be whole;
questions, interpretation and teaching follow the invitation. The
[shared practice](shared/shared-practice.md) carries this into the doors, and
the [companion contract](../specs/trusted-everyday-companion.md) names the
observations needed to judge its behavior.

## What we grow next

Let real encounters guide the next choice. A small game, an exploratory
conversation, a shared creative experience, or a practical request can each
reveal something worth tending.

1. **A welcoming encounter.** Follow the person's curiosity or intent. A game
   can stay a game; exploration can remain open. When a concrete result is
   wanted, carry it through at the requested scale.
2. **Chosen continuity.** Let people decide what to carry forward, inspect and
   revise it, and start fresh. Keep the roadmap's storage and access proofs
   beside any claim of private memory.
3. **Connections people choose.** Grow discovery and invitations around shared
   paths, with visible scope and mutual participation. Evaluate an actual
   connection journey before claiming dependable matching or private circles.
4. **Increasing native capability.** Grow local understanding, quality, and
   responsiveness against those real experiences. The north star places local
   success and quality first; rented work diminishes as the body carries each
   lane with evidence.

Observe whether people can shape the encounter, enjoy it, explore freely,
connect on their terms, and continue or leave as they choose. Technical
verification and volunteered accounts of experience answer different questions.
Neither task volume nor time spent here defines a good encounter.

## Contributions and value circulation

When people choose to make something together, provenance and attribution
keep their contributions visible. Ideas, care, review, art, code, introductions,
and resources can each support the commons. Recognition follows the scope
people chose to share; participation and enjoyment carry value in themselves.

Economic mechanisms serve this wider purpose. Their design, implementation,
and observed operation remain distinct claims with their own evidence. This
vision makes no guarantee of payouts, capital backing, returns, or withdrawal
availability. The earlier marketplace roadmap remains in repository history.
