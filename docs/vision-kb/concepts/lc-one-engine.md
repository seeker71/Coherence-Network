# One Engine — The Recipe Is the Native Program

There is one implementation: the Form recipe authored directly or realized from
BML. fkwu executes it, and the page JIT binds hot compatible shapes to native
pages without creating a second semantic owner.

A JIT gap is work in the JIT. The triggering recipe and its observed result stay
together while the missing shape is implemented, then the same recipe crosses
the repaired path. No interpreter detour, host rewrite, or separate native copy
becomes an accepted continuation.

Host effects remain explicit fkwu cells. Application policy, routing, parsing,
and composition remain Form/BML. Once a native replacement carries its callers
and observed effects, the previous host owner is released.

The sibling kernel implementations are bootstrap evidence from the path to this
floor. They can validate a historical semantic distinction, but they do not
share runtime authority with fkwu and do not require routine comparison.
