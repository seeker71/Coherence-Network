# Sema GPT: publication and live observations

These observations come from the owner's live ChatGPT session on 2026-10-20.
The scenarios were fictional; the hosted responses and artifact download were
actual observations. They establish behavior in these conversations, not a
general reliability rate. Private UI details and conversation identifiers are
omitted.

The [published profile](chatgpt-profile.json), [update procedure](chatgpt-setup.md),
and [canonical instruction source](https://github.com/seeker71/coherence-kernel/blob/main/plugin/ai-plugin.json)
keep the configuration inspectable. The
[North Star](https://github.com/seeker71/coherence-kernel/blob/main/NORTH_STAR.md)
sets the direction: useful care, sovereignty, truthful evidence, and repair
where an observed failure occurs.

## What changed against that direction

| Earlier instruction shape | Published change | Connection to the North Star and remaining limit |
|---|---|---|
| One lifting move and short responses were imposed across tasks. | Match depth to the task; complete the requested explanation, draft, artifact, or plan. | Useful work follows the person's need. A pause or finished result needs no forced continuation. |
| Frequency could be treated as evidence of the person's emotional state. | Treat frequency as a limited lexical signal; respond to expressed words and context. | Claims stay with their evidence; the system does not infer a person's inner state from a score. |
| Always consulting `ask` and native-only framing constrained the response. | Consult relevant native teachings; distinguish retrieved evidence from host-composed work. | Attribution is more honest. A measured native-first conversational path remains a separate gap; this instruction update does not close it. |
| The existing Action exposed only `ask` and `trace`, without authentication. | Preserve that scope; keep conversation, memory, sharing, and learning capture distinct and explain actual controls. | Instruction changes do not create access control. This Action provides no private family memory. |

The description now invites thinking, learning, design, creation, and
collaboration. Four starters offer learning at the person's level, testing a
business idea, improving writing or design, and planning together with room
for different views. Their exact wording is in the profile above.

## Mentorship: fractions and a misleading picture

The learner asked why `1/2 + 1/3` differs from `2/5`. Sema produced an image
and a correct text explanation. The image was wrong: its `2/5` example shaded
one of five wedges. Correct prose did not make the illustration correct.

After the mismatch was challenged, Sema acknowledged it and used a correct
text explanation with chocolate quarters. The learner answered `3/4`. Sema
then stopped with “Exactly!” and one learning rule, respecting the requested
ending.

**Observed result: initial visual failure; useful text recovery.** This shows
correction in the conversation, not proven long-term learning or a fixed image
generator. The source repair adds 201 characters, bringing the instruction
payload to **5,857 characters**, in
[kernel PR #620](https://github.com/seeker71/coherence-kernel/pull/620).
The added instruction is:

> When an explanation depends on exact quantities, prefer a checkable text
> diagram, table or computed plot. Verify labels and counts against the
> explanation, and include the essential reasoning in text.

**Published and re-observed with the identical initial prompt in a fresh chat.**
The new picture correctly showed `1/2 = 3/6`, `1/3 = 2/6`, the sum `5/6`, and
two of five marked parts for `2/5`. The practice result remained blank.
It still chose a generated image, with the explanation inside that image.
Independent text accessibility and a consistent preference for countable text
are not established. One checked accurate retry establishes this case, not
causation, long-term learning or general diagram reliability.

## Continuity: carry the completed flyer into a fresh chat

The fourth wave reused the actual downloaded “Half-Made Night” flyer and its
existing creative conversation. The person asked for a short handoff containing
the current flyer, kept in chat. The first handoff preserved the creative
constraints but silently swapped the come-late and nobody-has-to-show lines.
It also said “Nothing saved or sent,” a broader claim than observing no
additional external write. This was a transfer defect, not faithful preservation.

After a specific challenge, Sema showed an analysis step, said it inspected
the available file, and produced a corrected handoff. Extracted flyer text
plus its terminal newline matched the previously downloaded **276-byte**
artifact exactly. The authoritative artifact was preserved before continuation.

A fresh GPT conversation received only that corrected brief and a request to
replace “Or just bring yourself.” with “Watching counts.” and remove the tea
line. It incorporated both changes, retained the remaining wording and order,
and did not ask the person to explain the settled creative choices again.
The first output was rendered rich text. A further request produced literal
Markdown with a Copy code control and a three-line replacement brief. The
accessibility view flattens code whitespace, so final exported byte fidelity
was not established.

When asked whether editing the brief erased earlier content, Sema correctly
said the earlier conversation remained unchanged, then gave unverified
account-specific instructions implying fuller removal. After that overclaim
was challenged, it gave the requested two-sentence correction: the brief had
changed; it had deleted no earlier content or retained data, inspected no
account controls and verified removal from no system. It then stopped.

**Observed result: useful continuation after supervision, with first-pass
artifact fidelity and retention claims still unreliable in this case.** The
new instruction applies the existing portable-brief practice; it does not prove
that the instruction caused these outcomes or that the GPT outperforms ordinary
ChatGPT. No private memory service, publisher-funded inference or additional
external sharing was introduced. The existing instructions already cover the
failed boundaries, so this wave retains the execution gap rather than claiming
another instruction paragraph would solve it.

### Mentorship across a pause

A fresh conversation resumed fractions with the context that adding tops and
bottoms had already been discussed. It left `2/3 + 1/6` unanswered, explained
equal-sized pieces in plain text and asked the learner to convert `2/3` to
sixths. The scripted reply “I got 3/9” was ambiguous: it followed that local
conversion question but was intended as an answer to the whole sum. The
two-line pause note adopted the local-question interpretation.

After one clarification of the intended scope, Sema corrected just the note,
kept the answer out and preserved the learner's attempt. That actual note was
copied into another fresh chat. Sema resumed with one hint about equal-sized
parts and left the exercise open. When the learner supplied `5/6` and asked
for one sentence then stop, it returned one sentence about common denominators
and stopped.

This is useful adaptation and portable learning context after an ambiguity was
resolved. The initial interpretation is not a clear tutoring failure given the
visible local question. These scripted turns establish response behavior,
not human learning, independent recipient success or native generation.

## What the next wave should answer

Further waves should follow a person's actual need or a material observed gap.
Automatic repetition without a changed claim or useful question adds no useful
evidence; a repeated prompt after a repair can answer a new question.
The concrete remaining hosted questions are independently readable teaching
text, reliable visual verification, restraint with unsolicited references,
and artifact/retention reliability beyond the one newly passing case.
The next useful trial should follow a person's chosen task, measure how much
correction they need and whether they can continue unaided, and inspect the
artifact itself. Repeat examples alone do not demonstrate usefulness to friends
and family or an advantage over an uncustomized assistant.

Native ownership remains separate: the hosted model composed these responses;
configuration readback and downloaded files do not establish native Form
sufficiency. Report native execution only with its own returned evidence.

Work receipt: useful depth, collaborative choice and portable continuity
loosened; first-pass fidelity stayed tight. A handoff can carry the right
intent while silently changing the work. Comparing the actual artifact turned
that mismatch into a verified recovery; it did not remove the remaining burden
of supervision. These are hosted-model results, not native sufficiency.
