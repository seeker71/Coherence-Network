# listen-gold — feedback in the person's hands

Receive what the person actually says about a response. Apply it to the
current encounter. Their reading of how it landed is theirs; an agent's
self-label cannot stand in for it. A pause or a brief correction can be a
complete contribution.

## Choose the scope

Record a durable sample only when the person requests it or an established
authorization covers the selected content, destination and purpose. Learning
capture is distinct from memory, publication and ordinary conversation.
Prepare the exact sample first and resolve only missing scope. Reuse approval
already given for the same use.

The person's words can name what served them, what they would prefer, or a
boundary. Further explanation is optional. Preserve their wording when they
choose to retain it, and keep personal or third-party context within the
authorized audience.

## Use an observed recording path

The existing protocol uses `clear` and `fear` labels. These are compatibility
keys in the recording format; the person can describe their experience in
their own language. Map a selected reading only when its meaning fits the
available field and the person has authorized that use.

Inspect the available native command or witness route and its actual storage
and access behavior before writing. Verify the resulting record. A local
path establishes location; confidentiality, deletion and training each need
their own evidence. The current native entry is described in
[`skills/form-cli`](../form-cli/SKILL.md) and the repository's `AGENTS.md`.

## Retention and learning

Audio, transcripts, raw notes and derived labels each follow the person's
chosen scope and retention. Keep only the material needed for that use.
Further review or extraction requires the same authorized purpose. When
correction or deletion is requested, use the available control, verify its
result and name its limits.

A retained reading proves capture. A training receipt proves a particular
training operation. Later behavior establishes whether the lesson helped.
Report these effects separately. The encounter itself remains valuable
whether or not the person chooses to make a training sample.
