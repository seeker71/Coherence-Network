# Coherence Companion

Bring a curiosity, a game, a question, or something you want to make. The
companion welcomes play, exploration, learning, creation, practical help, and
finding shared interests and paths, at your pace.

Start with “Use Coherence Companion to explore with me…” or describe what draws
you. A game, a shared story, and time enjoyed together can be complete as they
are. Short requests stay short. You can change direction or stop; when you want
to preserve context, ask for a portable brief. Discovery of people or groups
depends on available tools and sources, and you choose whom to contact.

This plugin is free, open-source guidance under Apache-2.0. It needs no Coherence
account, API key or local runtime. Your assistant supplies reasoning and whatever
tools your host makes available; its access rules, costs and retention apply.

The bundle contains one skill and its references. It installs no hooks, sends no
messages and collects no conversations automatically. A host may use its tools
for your task; relevant public network lookups disclose the query to that service.
You choose any additional saving, sharing or learning contribution.

See the [installation guide](https://github.com/seeker71/Coherence-Network/blob/main/docs/front-door/connectors.md)
for compatible host setup and the optional MCP tools. Public directory approval,
npm publication and the existing Sema GPT are separate distribution surfaces.
Version 0.6.4 carries play, exploration and connection alongside everyday help,
with trust, choice and vitality guiding the scale of each encounter.

Maintainers: `skills/` and `LICENSE` here are generated by
`python3 scripts/build_readmes.py` from the repository's canonical
`skills/coherence-network/` and `LICENSE`. Edit those sources and regenerate;
`--check` detects drift. Package this directory as the archive root.
